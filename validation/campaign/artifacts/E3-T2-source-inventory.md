## Dataset Selection Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Data policy | Public datasets or synthetic data only; no customer PII |
| License | Must permit non-commercial or research use; attribution documented in E3-T6 |
| Domain fit | Must map to retail/e-commerce schema defined in E3-T1 |
| Volume | Combined load must reach 10–50 GB after transformation to curated schema |
| Privacy | No real individual identities; synthetic names/emails generated at load time |
| Sensitivity | All data classified as `public` or `synthetic` — no confidential classification |
